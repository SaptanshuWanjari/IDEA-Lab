# Cyber Incident Policy (pol-cyber-01)

Linked regulation: `cyber-controls-2026` · Owner: IT · Version: v1.4

## 1. Purpose

Implement the Cyber Controls Directions for Pravah Demo Bank: keep a
board-approved security baseline, detect and report incidents within mandated
timelines, harden privileged access, and prove control maturity through testing
and crisis exercises.

## 2. Procedure

| Step | Action | Owner | Maps to clause |
|------|--------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | Appoint the Chief Information Security Officer for cyber posture and incident-response readiness. | IT | 1.1 |
| 2 | Maintain the board-approved Information Security policy for internet-facing systems, privileged accounts, and remote admin access; review every 12 months. | IT | 1.2 |
| 3 | Report every cyber incident to the Reserve Bank within 6 hours of detection; submit the detailed root-cause report within 30 days of containment. | IT | 2.1 |
| 4 | Enforce multi-factor authentication for all privileged and remote admin access; disable single-factor access to critical systems within 60 days. | IT | 2.2 |
| 5 | Retain immutable audit logs of sensitive customer-information access for 12 months; security operations reviews anomalies daily. | IT | 2.3 |
| 6 | Run vulnerability assessment and penetration testing of internet-facing systems every 6 months; remediate criticals within 15 days. | IT | 2.4 |
| 7 | Segment critical systems from office networks; route inter-zone traffic through inspected deny-by-default gateways reviewed every 90 days. | IT | 2.5 |
| 8 | CISO reports incident trends and control maturity to the IT Strategy Committee every 3 months. | Risk | 3.1 |
| 9 | Conduct a yearly cyber crisis simulation (ransomware + data breach); remediate gaps within 90 days with retained evidence. | IT | 3.2 |

## 3. Evidence

Incident tickets + RBI reporting log, MFA enforcement report, audit-log sample, VA/PT reports, firewall rule review, committee minutes, simulation report.
