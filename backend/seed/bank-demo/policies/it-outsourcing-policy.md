# IT Outsourcing Policy (pol-outsource-01)

Linked regulation: `it-outsourcing-2026` | Owner: IT | Version: v1.0

## 1. Purpose

Implement the IT Outsourcing Directions: keep the central outsourcing register, risk-assess material arrangements for Board approval, diligence providers, contract audit rights and data ownership, manage concentration and exit strategies, enforce data localisation in India, control subcontracting, and audit material arrangements annually.

## 2. Control statement

Business owners evidence each control through MAPs linked to this policy; exceptions need documented approval.
