# AML/CFT Policy (pol-aml-01)

Linked regulation: `aml-cft-2026` | Owner: Compliance | Version: v1.0

## 1. Purpose

Implement the AML/CFT Directions: designate the MLRO, run the annual enterprise-wide risk assessment, diligence correspondent banking, capture wire-transfer particulars, monitor PEPs, deploy trade-based laundering rules, screen virtual-asset exposures, report attempted suspicious transactions, and retain records for 5 years.

## 2. Control statement

Business owners evidence each control through MAPs linked to this policy; exceptions need documented approval.
