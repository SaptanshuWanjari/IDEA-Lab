# Operational Resilience Policy (pol-ops-01)

Linked regulation: `op-resilience-2026` · Owner: Operations · Version: v1.0

## 1. Purpose

Implement the Operational Resilience Directions for Pravah Demo Bank: map
critical operations, set board-approved impact tolerances, meet RTO/RPO
targets, and demonstrate recovery through drills, backups, and yearly
self-assessment.

## 2. Procedure

| Step | Action | Owner | Maps to clause |
|------|--------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | Identify critical operations and map supporting people, processes, technology, and third parties within 6 months. | Operations | 1.1 |
| 2 | Set impact tolerances (maximum tolerable disruption) per critical operation; Board approves yearly. | Risk | 1.2 |
| 3 | Define RTO <= 4 hours and RPO <= 2 hours per critical operation; demonstrate recovery via testing every 6 months. | Operations | 2.1 |
| 4 | Maintain alternate processing sites (independent power/connectivity) able to assume critical operations within RTO; yearly switchover drills. | Operations | 2.2 |
| 5 | Assess material third-party resilience yearly; contracts carry audit rights and a 90-day termination clause for persistent failures. | Legal | 2.3 |
| 6 | Maintain the business continuity plan with communication protocols; activate for disruptions expected beyond 2 hours; notify the Reserve Bank within 24 hours. | Operations | 2.4 |
| 7 | Keep backup copies of critical data in two geographically separate locations; test restoration for at least one critical operation every 3 months rotationally. | IT | 2.5 |
| 8 | Risk Management Committee reviews resilience test outcomes every 6 months; Operations reports tolerance breaches with remediation timelines. | Risk | 3.1 |
| 9 | Run a full-scope resilience self-assessment yearly; remediate high deficiencies within 90 days with retained closure evidence. | Audit | 3.2 |

## 3. Evidence

Critical-operations register, tolerance approvals, RTO/RPO test reports, drill log, vendor assessments, BCP + RBI notification log, backup restoration log, self-assessment pack.
