# Customer Data Privacy Policy (pol-privacy-01)

Linked regulation: `data-privacy-2026` | Owner: Compliance | Version: v1.0

## 1. Purpose

Implement the Customer Data Privacy Directions: appoint the Data Protection Officer, keep the data inventory, enforce consent and purpose limitation, honour access/correction/erasure requests in 30 days, encrypt and tokenise data, notify breaches in 24/72 hours, schedule retention and anonymisation, govern third-party sharing, and assess new products before launch.

## 2. Control statement

Business owners evidence each control through MAPs linked to this policy; exceptions need documented approval.
