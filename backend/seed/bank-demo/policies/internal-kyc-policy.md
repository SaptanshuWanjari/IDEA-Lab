# Internal KYC Policy (pol-kyc-01)

Linked regulation: `kyc-aml-2026` · Owner: Compliance · Version: v2.1

## 1. Purpose

Implement the KYC/AML Directions for Pravah Demo Bank: complete customer due
diligence before account activation, refresh KYC on schedule, monitor
transactions, file regulatory reports on time, and evidence everything for
supervisory inspection.

## 2. Procedure

| Step | Action | Owner | Maps to clause |
|------|--------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | Designate the Principal Officer for customer identification and record-keeping. | Compliance | 1.1 |
| 2 | Complete Customer Due Diligence for every new relationship before activation; record identity/address proof in the central KYC registry. | Operations | 1.2 |
| 3 | Run periodic KYC updation: high-risk every 2 years, medium-risk every 8 years, low-risk every 10 years. | Operations | 1.3 |
| 4 | Refresh KYC for high-risk accounts within 30 days of a risk-category change; file the monthly overdue-refresh exception report to the Risk Management Committee. | Compliance | 2.1 |
| 5 | Monitor transactions for complexity/unusual patterns; retain account and transaction records for 5 years. | Risk | 2.2 |
| 6 | File Cash Transaction Reports above Rs. 10 lakh and Suspicious Transaction Reports within 7 days of concluding suspicion; Principal Officer approves each filing. | Compliance | 2.3 |
| 7 | Screen customers/beneficial owners against UNSC and domestic watchlists at onboarding and daily; freeze matches without prior notice. | Risk | 2.4 |
| 8 | Board review of KYC/AML compliance every 6 months with CRO trend analysis of alerts, STRs, and overdue refreshes. | Legal | 3.1 |
| 9 | Independent audit of KYC/AML controls every 12 months; close findings within 90 days with retained evidence. | Audit | 3.2 |

## 3. Evidence

KYC registry extract, exception report CSV, STR/CTR filing log, screening log, board minutes, audit closure pack.
